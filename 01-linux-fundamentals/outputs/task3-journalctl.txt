############ 0. What is journalctl reading? ############
$ systemctl is-active systemd-journald
active
>> systemd-journald is the logging daemon. It collects messages from the kernel,
>> from initrd, from service stdout/stderr and from syslog, and stores them in a
>> BINARY, INDEXED journal. 'journalctl' is the query tool for that journal.

$ ls /var/log/journal/*
system.journal

$ journalctl --disk-usage
Archived and active journals take up 8.0M in the file system.
