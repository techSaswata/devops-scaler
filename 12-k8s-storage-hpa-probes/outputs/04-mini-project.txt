=== 6. ALL REPLICAS SHARE THE SAME PVC ===

$ kubectl get pods -n miniproject -l app=webapp -o custom-columns=POD:.metadata.name,NODE:.spec.nodeName --no-headers
webapp-9f679f46f-f8nzb   devops-hw-worker
webapp-9f679f46f-nm8lq   devops-hw-worker
webapp-9f679f46f-rrbrr   devops-hw-worker
webapp-9f679f46f-zxrg5   devops-hw-worker
>> NOTE: accessModes is ReadWriteOnce, which is per-NODE. These replicas
>> could only all mount it because local-path scheduled them onto the same
>> node. On a real cluster, scaling a Deployment that shares one RWO volume
>> will leave pods stuck in ContainerCreating on other nodes - this is why
>> stateful workloads use a StatefulSet with volumeClaimTemplates instead.
