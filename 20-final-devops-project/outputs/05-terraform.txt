=== 11. NOTHING OF MINE LEFT ANYWHERE IN THE REGION ===
$ aws resourcegroupstaggingapi get-resources --tag-filters Key=Owner,Values=24BCS10248
  indexed: arn:aws:ec2:ap-south-1:043848616679:instance/i-0e2802a42c9036026

--- An ARN here does NOT prove the resource exists. The tagging API is
--- an INDEX, and it keeps entries for terminated resources for a while
--- after the resource itself is reaped. Treating this list as the truth
--- would mean either a false alarm or, worse, trusting it the other way
--- round. So each ARN is resolved against the service that owns it:
  i-0e2802a42c9036026 -> gone; no instance record at all (stale index entry)
